- `rlm.spawn(target="sandbox")` sized every bot sandbox at 2 vCPU and 4 GB. The spawn now takes `cpu` and `memory_gb` (whole numbers, sandbox targets only): the daemon passes them to the sandbox create and refuses sizes above `bots.maxCpu` (default 16) or `bots.maxMemoryGb` (default 32), the small defaults included. A bot keeps its recorded size across a checkpoint restore and a registry rebuilt from labels. A nested bot enforces its owner's caps. The /bots row prices the bot by its size.
