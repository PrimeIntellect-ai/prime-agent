- `rlm.spawn(target="sandbox")` sized every bot sandbox at 2 vCPU and 4 GB. The spawn now takes `cpu` and `memory_gb` (whole numbers, sandbox targets only): the daemon passes them to the sandbox create, refuses sizes above `bots.maxCpu` (default 16) or `bots.maxMemoryGb` (default 32), keeps the small defaults when omitted, restores a bot at its recorded size, and prices the /bots row by the bot's size.
