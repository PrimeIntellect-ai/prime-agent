- Bots now work end to end on a live Prime account. The link to a bot no longer ends before the bot's daemon says hello (the relay was started with an already-expired deadline). `make bot-tarball` builds a linux-x64 tarball of the checkout next to the binary, and the daemon installs it in the bot, so a local build can run bots. A bot whose prime-agent has no bot guest side fails the spawn with a clear message, a relay that ends before the hello reports why, and platform rate limits (HTTP 429) are retried.
